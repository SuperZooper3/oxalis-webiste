What's the problem?

The blood supply isn't reliable.

Low Eligibility

Only 50% of people in Canada are eligible to donate blood due to medications, cancer, diseases or other health issues.

Even Lower Donations

In Canada, only 1.23% of people actually donate blood. At any given time, there is less than 5 days supply of blood.

Supplys are Low

Blood expires very quickly meaning stocks are temporary. In times of crisis donations fall even lower, putting the lives at risk.
