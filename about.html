Hello! This is Oxalis.

A company working to solve blood.

Who Are We?

We are a team of enthusiastic teenagers trying to drive real change in the blood sector.

Our vision is to make it easier for patients to receive the medical supplies they need, whenever that might be! We want a world where no one dies or misses surgeries because there are not enough biomedical supplies.
We plan to execute on this vision through multiple steps:

  • Synthesize red blood cell substitutes (done with ABOT).
  • Synthesize all components of whole blood.
  • Replace human blood donors for plasma.
  • Expand to synthesize even more natural products from the body.
